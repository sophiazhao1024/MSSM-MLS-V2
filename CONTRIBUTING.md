# Contributions and attribution

The research motivation was the difficulty of scanning MSSM parameters with previous MCMC work, followed by development of MLS with collaborators. The originating MSSM research contribution centred on participation in the MLS implementation and analysis of its conclusions. That research contribution should be described accurately in interviews and publications; it is not a claim that every general machine-learning component, external physics library, later reconstruction or packaging file was personally authored by that researcher.

This repository is a new reconstructed/updated software project. The supplied V5 GP-assisted implementation and engineering controls are distinct from the lost original author program. Preserve paper and upstream citations, the HiggsSignals-derived driver notice and all external licences. Record actual contributors to new changes; do not automatically list the paper authors as authors of unrelated new implementation files.

For changes, use a new named branch/run, retain source/configuration hashes and old failures, and update tests and scientific evidence separately. Software tests do not establish better physical scan efficiency. A new physical profile or method needs a fresh explicit comparison; do not modify this V5 snapshot's archived numbers or reclassify the historical failed development gate.
